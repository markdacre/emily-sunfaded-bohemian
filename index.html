How we begin · three quiet steps

No templates. No rush. Just a plan that fits the body you live in.

01

We talk first

A free 20-minute call — Zoom, WhatsApp, whatever you prefer. You tell me what's going on: the injury, the ache, the run you've been missing, the grandkid you can't lift. I listen, ask a lot of questions, and tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.

02

We sketch your plan

Your program is written by hand, for you, the week before we start — never a template. If you've been told to "just be careful" or "stop doing the thing you love", that's exactly where we begin, and we work back toward it, week by week.

03

We get to work

We train together — in the Cascais studio, on a Lisbon balcony, in your living room in Marin. I adjust the plan as your body teaches us things. My job is to make you more capable in your own life, then to gracefully step out of the way once you are.

About · the person behind the plan

Hi, I'm Emily.

I grew up under California sun — long beach days, hand-me-down bicycles, the kind of childhood that quietly teaches you to trust your body. I've spent the last decade coaching people of every age and ability: athletes chasing one more season, parents trying to keep up, folks rebuilding after surgery, retirees who simply want to walk down to the market without thinking about their hip.

Two years ago I followed a long-held dream across the Atlantic and opened a small studio in Cascais. I work slowly, ask a lot of questions, and adjust the plan as your body teaches us things. The goal hasn't changed — to make you more capable in your own life. The light is just better here.
